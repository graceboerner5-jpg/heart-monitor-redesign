# Problem Statement

Current wearable ECG devices require the user to remain still during monitoring which limits their use during physical activity.

## Background

Although heart monitoring devices have existed through many iterations, they continue to struggle with motion and muscle artifacts (MA). MA is the unwanted electrical signals from muscular signals or the device itself that confound the desired signal. While motion artifacts are simpler to eliminate, muscle artifacts can be more difficult because of their closeness in frequency to the heart signal. There have been many approaches to reduce muscle artifacts. However, current wearable ECG devices still require the user to remain still while in use, demonstrating how an effective way to eliminate muscle artifact has yet to be found.
